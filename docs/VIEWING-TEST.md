# Short viewing test

Use a separate browser profile for a fresh match so the test leaves the live checkpoint intact. Start with [Boulder and Zip at kickoff](http://127.0.0.1:41730/?seed=zeerush&audio=muted&session=new). Show the first 25 seconds in a phone-sized landscape view. The default seed reaches its first save at about 14 seconds. Follow with a short sound-on segment and [the halftime report](../artifacts/halftime.png).

With guardian approval, ask a small group from the intended age range to watch. Avoid explaining the two captains or the joke before showing the clips. Ask the same three questions after each viewing.

1. Who were you cheering for, and how could you tell them apart?
2. Where did the ball go, and who saved it or took it away?
3. Which moment was funny or surprising? What happened just before it?

Record which character and play children can describe without prompting, and where they lost track of the ball or the joke. Keep a note of whether the sound was on and whether the screen was small. These observations help choose the next camera or animation change. Chrome verification covers the visual and audio cues. Child audience feedback has yet to be collected.
