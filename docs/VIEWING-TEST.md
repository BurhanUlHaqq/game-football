# Short viewing test

Use a separate browser profile for a fresh match. Show the first 33 seconds of the grandstand-18 fixture in a phone-sized landscape view. It features a goal at 00:09, a wide shot at 00:22 and a high shot at 00:27. Then play the same sequence with sound. [Muted fixture](http://127.0.0.1:41730/?seed=grandstand-18&audio=muted&session=new) · [Sound-on recording](../artifacts/rush-arena-goal-line-preview.mp4)

With guardian approval, ask a small group from the intended age range to watch. Avoid explaining the two captains or the joke before showing the clips. Ask the same three questions after each viewing.

1. Who were you cheering for, and how could you tell them apart?
2. Which ball went in, which missed, and who threw the ball back?
3. Which moment was funny or surprising? What happened just before it?

Record which character and play children can describe without prompting, and where they lost track of the ball or the joke. Keep a note of whether the sound was on and whether the screen was small.
